==============================================================================
Función principal de la app
==============================================================================
FORGEN is a fitness training, nutrition, and progress app. Users log workouts,
body metrics, and meals in the app. Health Connect is an optional Profile
setting so users can (1) copy recent weight and body-fat readings from Health
Connect into their FORGEN profile, and (2) write workouts they finish in
FORGEN back to Health Connect for other health apps.

FORGEN es una app de entrenamiento, nutrición y progreso. Health Connect es
opcional, en Perfil: importar peso y grasa corporal recientes, y exportar
entrenos completados. No leemos historial de salud anterior a 30 días.

Cómo probarlo
1. Abre FORGEN → Perfil → Apple Salud / Health Connect.
2. Pulsa Conectar (Health Connect) y concede peso y grasa corporal.
3. Sincronizar métricas propone actualizar el perfil con lecturas de ≤30 días.
4. Activa “Exportar entrenamientos”, completa un entreno; aparece en Health Connect.

==============================================================================
Por cada permiso (inglés; Play suele revisar en inglés)
==============================================================================

READ Weight
Purpose: Read the user’s latest body weight from Health Connect.
Why: FORGEN uses current body weight for calorie estimates, nutrition targets,
and progress charts. Importing from Health Connect avoids duplicate manual entry.
User benefit: One tap to keep the in-app profile aligned with their scale.
Lookback: last 30 days only. We do not request Health Data History.

READ Body fat
Purpose: Read the user’s latest body-fat percentage from Health Connect.
Why: Body-fat is a progress metric in the profile. Users who track it on a
smart scale can import the newest reading instead of typing it.
User benefit: Faster, more accurate body-composition tracking in FORGEN.
Lookback: last 30 days only. We do not request Health Data History.

WRITE Exercise
Purpose: Write a workout session after the user finishes it in FORGEN.
Why: Users want completed gym, run, or Hyrox sessions to appear alongside
activity from other apps (Google Fit, watches, etc.).
User benefit: A single Health Connect timeline of all training.
Only sessions the user completes in FORGEN; optional toggle in Profile.

WRITE Active calories burned
Purpose: Attach active calories estimated for that FORGEN workout.
Why: Health Connect exercise records include energy so other apps can show
training load. We write calories for the session just completed, not a daily total.
User benefit: Other health apps see the effort of the FORGEN workout.

WRITE Distance
Purpose: Attach distance for running/walking sessions logged in FORGEN.
Why: Runner workouts include GPS or manual distance; writing it completes the
exercise record. Strength-only workouts omit distance.
User benefit: Run distance from FORGEN appears in Health Connect.

DO NOT DECLARE
Health Data History (READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY)
Background health data
Steps, heart rate, sleep, nutrition, or any other Health Connect type.

Respuestas cortas (español, si el formulario está en ES)
==============================================================================

¿Por qué Weight?
Para copiar el peso reciente (máx. 30 días) al perfil y calcular calorías y
progreso. El usuario confirma la importación. No leemos historial antiguo.

¿Por qué Body fat?
Para copiar el % de grasa reciente al perfil de progreso. El usuario confirma.
No leemos historial antiguo.

¿Por qué Exercise / calorías / distancia?
Al terminar un entreno en FORGEN, si el usuario activó “Exportar
entrenamientos”, escribimos esa sesión (tipo de ejercicio, calorías activas y
distancia si aplica) para que otras apps de salud la vean.

¿Por qué NO History?
Health Connect ya permite leer los últimos 30 días. FORGEN solo importa
muestras de ese periodo. Pedir History era innecesario y se eliminó del
manifiesto.
